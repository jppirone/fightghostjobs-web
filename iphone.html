iPhone Shortcut

Search from a screenshot on your iPhone.

FGJ Share is a Shortcut for the iPhone. You take a screenshot of a job card and share it to the Shortcut. The Shortcut reads the text in the screenshot and opens our search page, which puts what it found into the boxes. You check the boxes and press Search.

The first time you run it

Your iPhone asks two questions the first time.

  • It asks whether the Shortcut may open the FightGhostJobs website. Tap Allow.
  • It asks about deleting the screenshot, with two choices. Delete removes the screenshot this time, and the question comes back each time you run the Shortcut. Delete Always removes the screenshot this time, and the question does not come back on later runs.

After you approve, the Shortcut deletes your most recent screenshot. You can get it back from Recently Deleted on your phone.

How to use it

  1. Take a screenshot that shows the whole job card: the company, the job title, and any requisition number or KIO ID.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose FGJ Share.
  4. Read the text in the alert. If it shows personal information, tap Cancel.
  5. Otherwise continue. Our search page opens with the boxes filled.
  6. Check the boxes.
  7. Press Search.

What it cannot do

  • It cannot know what is in your screenshot. The reader looks only for the details on a job card: the company, the job title, the requisition number and the KIO ID. It can still pick up other text that it was not meant to read.
  • It cannot check your screenshot for you. It is up to you to make sure the screenshot shows only a job opening. Before the Shortcut opens our page, it shows you the text it read, with a Cancel button.
  • It cannot search for you. Nothing is searched until you press the normal Search button.
  • It cannot always find an opening. When it cannot, our page asks you to type the company and the job title, the requisition number or the KIO ID.

What happens to the text

The Shortcut reads the text in the screenshot and opens our search page with that text after the # sign in the web address. Our search page puts that text into the boxes. Nothing is searched, and nothing is sent to us, until you press the normal Search button. The address with the text in it may stay in your browser history on your phone. We never receive the screenshot. See our privacy policy.

How to remove it

Delete the FGJ Share Shortcut in the Shortcuts app.
