What each tier includes

FightGhostJobs is a registry of job postings disclosed by employers. This page lists what the free tier and the destination links tier include at the moment.

Free on every tier

  • Title, location, dates and postID
  • AI-use disclosure: filtering and interviewing, shown separately
  • Recruiter involved: yes or no
  • A go-live time you schedule, and a dashboard to manage your postings

Destination links tier

Everything in the free tier, and:

  • Destination links, up to 10 per posting, that a candidate who chooses to apply is sent to
  • The option to name the recruiting firm on a posting
  • The option to state that applications are accepted only through the destination links

Each address is the one the employer enters. FightGhostJobs has not verified it.

Pricing is not published yet. To ask about the destination links tier, email sales@fightghostjobs.com.
