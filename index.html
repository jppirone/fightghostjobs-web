Not another way to apply faster

Mass-applying and mass-filtering got us here. We're not adding a faster gun to that arms race.

An application takes time — on average 31 minutes5, and a full job search adds up to roughly 51 hours of it5. Spray-and-pray spends that time on postings that may never lead to a hire; AI-filtering spends it screening out people a person never saw. FightGhostJobs bets on the alternative: candidates who apply with intention to postings where the employer has disclosed the facts, and employers who'd rather be found by the right five people than buried under five hundred.

One link, not five guesses

Bogus sites borrow real company names. We give everyone one place to check the link the employer named.

Scam and copycat postings are common enough that 95% of job seekers have hit a suspicious listing or offer3. FightGhostJobs gives both sides a single, plain reference: this is the listing, and this is the link the employer named as the place to apply.
