Not another way to apply faster

Mass-applying and mass-filtering got us here. We're not adding a faster gun to that arms race.

A real application takes real time — on average 31 minutes, and a full job search adds up to roughly 51 hours of it5. Spray-and-pray spends that time on postings that were never real; AI-filtering spends it screening out people a person never saw. FightGhostJobs bets on the alternative: candidates who apply with intention to postings that are verified, and employers who'd rather be found by the right five people than buried under five hundred.
