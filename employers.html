For employers

Prove your posting is real. Then stop drowning in the wrong applicants.

Candidate skepticism about ghost jobs is now a tax on every real posting, including yours — and it's not the only cost. 24% of employers had at least one fake or fraudulent applicant in the past year1, and 56% call "too many unqualified applicants" their top hiring problem, four years running2. A verified listing, with an apply link that only a confirmed real person can reach, works against both problems at once.

Only 8% call it fair

Candidates don't trust the process, and honestly, the math backs them up.

Only 8% of candidates believe AI makes hiring more fair3, and 46% of U.S. job seekers say their trust in hiring has dropped in the past year3. Meanwhile recruiters are drowning too — 34% now spend up to half their week just filtering spam3. Neither side is winning the mass-apply-versus-mass-filter arms race. FightGhostJobs isn't a faster filter — it's a way to opt out of the race entirely by showing candidates your postings are real before they ever apply, which is the kind of thing that actually rebuilds trust instead of automating around it.
