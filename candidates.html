Your time is the actual cost

A real application takes 31 minutes. Spent on a listing that was never real, that's 31 minutes gone for nothing.

The average job seeker spends roughly 51 hours on applications alone over a search4 — and 47% have applied to a job that turned out not to exist at all5. FightGhostJobs isn't built to help you fire off more applications faster. It's built so the ones you do send go somewhere real, to an employer who's shown their work — which is the opposite of spray-and-pray, and we think that's worth favoring.
